# Limiel Insure Hub

Build a modern, responsive insurance marketplace website for Limiel Insurance. The design should be inspired by leading African insurance comparison websites but must use a unique layout, branding, typography, icons, and UI components.

Brand Name:

Limiel Insurance

Theme Colors:

- Primary: #0056D2

- Secondary: #00A99D

- Accent: #F9A826

- Background: #F8FAFC

- Dark Text: #1F2937

The website should include:

1. Hero Section

- Large background image

- Headline:

"Compare Insurance Plans in Minutes"

- Subheading:

"Get affordable Motor, Health, Travel, Life and Business Insurance from trusted providers."

- CTA Buttons:

Get Quote

Compare Plans

2. Navigation

- Home

- Products

- Insurance Providers

- Claims

- About

- Blog

- Contact

- Login

Sticky navigation with mobile hamburger menu.

3. Insurance Categories

Cards for:

- Motor Insurance

- Health Insurance

- Travel Insurance

- Life Insurance

- Medical Cover

- Home Insurance

- Business Insurance

- Personal Accident

Each card should contain:

- modern icon

- short description

- Learn More button

4. Quote Comparison Section

Interactive quote form with:

- Insurance Type

- Age

- Vehicle Details

- Location

- Budget

- Submit

Display comparison cards showing:

Company Logo

Monthly Premium

Coverage

Benefits

Apply Button

5. Why Choose Limiel

Four animated cards:

- Fast Quotes

- Trusted Insurers

- Affordable Premiums

- Instant Support

6. Insurance Partners

Scrolling logos

7. Customer Testimonials

8. Latest Blog Articles

9. FAQ Accordion

10. Contact Section

11. Newsletter

12. Footer

Social media

Quick Links

Products

Privacy Policy

Terms

- React

- Framer Motion animations

- Beautiful gradients

- Glassmorphism cards

- Modern shadows
